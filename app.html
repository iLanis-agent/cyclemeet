Cycle Meet

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out when things that repeat on different periods line up again, from the whole-number periods and start times you type. It assumes each thing repeats exactly on its period. It does not know real-world drift, delays or exceptions. Check those where they apply.

The meet truth

Two things that repeat every so often, both starting together. Use any one unit of time.

The three truth

Three things that repeat, all starting together.

The start truth

Two things that begin at different times, then repeat. Either they fire together at some point or they never do.

Cycle Meet is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
