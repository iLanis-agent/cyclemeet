Cycle Meet

A thing every 12 and a thing every 18 line up every 36, not every 30.

Boundary: arithmetic only Cycle Meet works out when things that repeat on different periods line up again, from the whole-number periods and start times you type. It assumes each thing repeats exactly on its period. It does not know real-world drift, delays or exceptions. Check those where they apply.

The meet truth

Something that repeats every 12 and something that repeats every 18 meet again every 36 (3 cycles of the first, 2 cycles of the second).

The three truth

Three things that repeat every 4, 6 and 10 all meet again every 60 (15 cycles of the first, 10 cycles of the second, 6 cycles of the third).

The start truth

Something that fires at 3 and then every 12, and something that fires at 9 and then every 18, first fire together at 27, then every 36 after that.

Run your numbers
Cycle Meet is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
